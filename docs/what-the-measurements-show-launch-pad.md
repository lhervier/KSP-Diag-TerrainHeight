# What the measurements show: a launch pad of Making History

Part of [KSP Diag - Terrain Height](../README.md): what [the measurements of a launch pad of Making History](the-measurements-launch-pad.md)
say.

**The height KSP computes does not move.** The same digits at all six launches: the ground the game
flattens around the launch site, 820 m above the sea.

**The deck the craft stands on is not at the same height from one session to the next.** It spreads
over 592.8 mm. Two sessions often find it at the same height, to the thousandth of a millimetre — four
of the six here — but not all of them.

**The game sets the launch pad on the ground differently from one session to the next.** In the five
sessions where its deck stands higher, the launch pad wrote that it moved itself up so that its legs
stand above the ground, and by how much; in the session where its deck stands lowest, it wrote nothing.
On the screenshots, its frame stands above the sand in those five sessions, on the sand in session 5.

**Its feet stand on the ground at every launch.** Whether the launch pad moved itself up or not, it
stretches its legs down to the ground: the screenshots show each foot on the sand, neither above it
nor sunk into it. What changes is the height of everything above the feet, the deck the craft stands
on included.
